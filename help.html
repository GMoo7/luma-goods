Shipping

Orders ship from our warehouse in 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 4–6 business days and is free on orders over $90; below that it costs $8. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days for $18.

Returns

Send anything back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items should be unused and in their original packaging. Start a return from your order confirmation email and we’ll send a prepaid label.

Care instructions

Waxed canvas: spot clean with a damp cloth; never machine wash. Leather: condition twice a year and keep away from direct heat. Linen and merino: wash cold on a gentle cycle and dry flat. Stoneware: dishwasher and microwave safe.
